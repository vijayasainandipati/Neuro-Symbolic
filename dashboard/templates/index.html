📈 Accuracy Improvement Techniques

To achieve 90%+ accuracy, the system uses:

  1. Transfer Learning — ResNet50 pretrained on ImageNet, fine-tuned for landslide detection
  2. Data Augmentation — Random flips, rotations, brightness adjustments during training
  3. Ensemble Models — Average predictions from multiple architectures:
    Prediction = 0.5 × (CNN output) + 0.5 × (Transformer output)
