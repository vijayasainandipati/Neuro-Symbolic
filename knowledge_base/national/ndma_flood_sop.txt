# NDMA Flood Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)
Document ID: NDMA_FL_SOP_2026_01
Source: National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)
Authority: Tier 1
Hazard: Flood
District: All-India / Tamil Nadu
Language: English
Issued Date: 2026-08-01
Expiry Date: 2026-12-31
Section: Evacuation Protocols

1. Mandatory Evacuation Procedures
When water levels breach danger thresholds in coastal or low-lying reach areas (designated Zone A), local authorities must enforce mandatory evacuation before daylight expiration (standard 18:00 IST cutoff). 
Evacuation routes must use designated highway corridors (e.g. State Highway 44). Compromised bridges or submerged culverts must be immediately closed with physical barricades.

2. Community Broadcast Safety
Evacuation orders supersede all previous advisory levels. Local community messages advising citizens to remain in active inundation zones must be officially contradicted to prevent life safety hazards.
