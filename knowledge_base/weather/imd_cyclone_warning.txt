Language: English

Section: Weather Forecast & Landfall Alert

1. Severe Cyclonic Storm Varun
Severe Cyclonic Storm Varun is centered over the Southwest Bay of Bengal. Landfall is projected along the southern Tamil Nadu coast between 19:00 and 21:00 IST today.
Gale wind speeds reaching 110-120 km/h gusting to 135 km/h expected. Heavy to extremely heavy rainfall forecast across Kanyakumari District.
Fishermen are strictly advised not to venture into deep sea waters.
