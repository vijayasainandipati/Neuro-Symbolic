# Central Water Commission (CWC) River Flood Advisory
Document ID: CWC_FL_2026_03
Source: Central Water Commission (CWC)
Authority: Tier 3
Hazard: Flood
District: Kanyakumari
Language: English
Issued Date: 2026-08-29
Expiry Date: 2026-08-30
Section: Hydrological Inundation Forecast

1. River Discharge & Gauge Inundation
Water levels in the coastal river drainage systems are rising rapidly at 0.4 meters per hour due to heavy inflow from Pechiparai catchment areas.
Zone A low-lying residential sectors adjacent to the river mouth face imminent inundation.
North River Bridge pier scour has reached danger marks; bridge closure enforced by traffic police is valid and imperative for vehicle safety.
Evacuation corridor via State Highway 44 (SH-44) remains elevated and clear of flood waters.
