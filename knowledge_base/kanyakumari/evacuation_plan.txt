1. Zone A Mandatory Evacuation Order #KA-204
Due to Cyclone Varun storm surge and river backflow, mandatory evacuation of Zone A (coastal lowlands) is ordered effective immediately.
All residents must complete transit before 18:00 IST (6:00 PM) today.
Designated green corridor: State Highway 44 (SH-44) Northbound.
North River Bridge is structurally compromised and closed to all vehicular traffic.

2. Transit Assistance
Special government transport buses and volunteer shuttles are deployed at Zone A Central Bus Stand.
Citizens requiring medical evacuation or elderly transit should report to Zone A staging post or send SOS via emergency mesh.
