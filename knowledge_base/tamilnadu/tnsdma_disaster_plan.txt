# Tamil Nadu State Disaster Management Authority (TNSDMA) Response Plan
Document ID: TNSDMA_RESP_2026_01
Source: Tamil Nadu State Disaster Management Authority (TNSDMA)
Authority: Tier 1
Hazard: Cyclone & Monsoon Flooding
District: Kanyakumari
Language: English / Tamil
Issued Date: 2026-08-20
Expiry Date: 2026-12-31
Section: District Response Protocol

1. Inter-Agency Coordination
The District Collector as Chairman of DDMA holds sovereign command over all disaster relief, transit corridors, and official public warnings.
State Disaster Response Force (SDRF) units are placed under direct operational control of District Emergency Operation Centers (DEOC).

2. Relief Camp Standards
All designated multipurpose cyclone shelters and school-based relief shelters must provide dry rations, potable drinking water, clean sleeping mats, baby formula, and emergency power backup.
Intake registers must be maintained 24/7.
